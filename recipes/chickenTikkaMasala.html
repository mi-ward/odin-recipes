Chicken Tikka Masala

Description

I like Chicken Tikka Masala a lot. I didn't have Indian food until I moved to New York. I've tried making it at home and I always do a terrible job. I haven't tried this recipe but maybe it's good.

One day I'll devote myself to actually making a few different batches to find out what works and what doesn't.

Ingredients

For The Chicken Tikka

  • 400 g Chicken Breast 14oz
  • 125 g Natural Yoghurt 1 Cup
  • 6 Garlic Cloves
  • 1 tablespoon Grated Ginger
  • 1 tablespoon Kashmiri Chilli Powder
  • 1 teaspoon Ground Turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon Ground Coriander
  • 1 teaspoon Ground Cumin
  • ½ teaspoon Amchur
  • ½ teaspoon Ground Fenugreek
  • ½ teaspoon Salt

For the Masala Sauce:

  • 1 tablespoon Ghee
  • 1 Small Onion 100g
  • 2 Red Chillies
  • 12 Curry Leaves
  • ¼ teaspoon Ground Cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon Ground Cloves
  • 1 teaspoon Garam Masala
  • 1 tablespoon Dried Fenugreek Leaves
  • 250 ml Tomato Passata 1 Cup
  • 75 ml Water ⅓ Cup
  • 1 tablespoon Tomato Puree
  • 1 tablespoon Sugar
  • ¼ teaspoon Salt
  • 50 ml Double 3 tablespoon + 1 teaspoon (Heavy cream in the US)

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken into 2-3cm (~1") cubes.
  2. Take the garlic and ginger and mash to form a paste, adding a little water if required.
  3. Mix this garlic and ginger paste with the remaining ingredients for the chicken tikka.
  4. Massage to coat well and then allow to marinate for at least 4 hours and up to 24 hours.
  5. Chop the onion for the masala sauce into a 1cm (~½") dice.
  6. Cut the red chillies in half lengthways.
  7. Heat the ghee in a 28cm or 11" frying pan over a medium-high heat. Add the slit red chilli peppers and curry leaves and cook for a minute.
  8. Add the onion and fry until golden, stirring occasionally; this should take around 10 minutes.
  9. Add the tomato puree and pour in the tomato passata and water, followed by the sugar, salt, ground cinnamon, ground cloves, garam masala and fenugreek leaves.
  10. Reduce the heat to low and simmer while preparing the chicken tikka (around 20 minutes).
  11. Meanwhile, heat your grill/broiler to high and thread your chicken onto skewers.
  12. Scrape any remaining marinade into the sauce, stir it in and allow it to continue gently simmering.
  13. When the broiler is hot, place the chicken tikka skewers under and cook for a total of 15 minutes, turning when each side is nicely coloured.
  14. Whilst the tikka is cooking, blend the sauce thoroughly and then pass it through a fine-mesh sieve.
  15. Return the sauce to the pan and stir through the cream.
  16. Add the chicken, simmer in the sauce for 3-4 minutes, and serve.
