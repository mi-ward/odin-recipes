New York Pizza

Description

I tried making pizza at home during the pandemic. I think I've watched enough videos now to do a pretty good job.

Soon enough I'll try making a homemade pizza again. Might even try on the grill.

Ingredients

Dough Ingredients

  • Bread Flour - 284g
  • Water - 176g
  • Yeast - 1.1g
  • Salt - 7g
  • Sugar - 6
  • Olive Oil - 9g

Pizza Ingredients

  • Mozzarella Cheese (fresh, grated)
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Pepperoni

Steps

Dough Instructions

  1. Cool water to < 60°F.
  2. Mix the water and active dry yeast.
  3. Add flour and Olive Oil and mix for 2 minutes.
  4. Keep mixing on low, then add Sugar and Salt.
  5. Mix for 10 more minutes.
  6. Cover the dough with plastic wrap and rest for 1-3 hours.
  7. Divide the dough into portions.
  8. Shape into balls, sealing the seam.
  9. Put the dough in a lightly oiled container.
  10. Cover tightly and refrigerate for 2 - 4 days. 3 days is ideal.
  11. Let the dough come to room temperature before using.

Pizza Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 550
  2. Spread dough to form pizza
  3. Add sauce
  4. Add cheese
  5. Place in oven for 7 minutes
